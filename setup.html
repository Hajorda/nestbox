Welcome to Nestbox

A few questions, about two minutes. You can change everything later in the control panel.
📍 Nestbox searches Brussels and the area around it and has only been tested there. The areas, commute estimates and message texts are Brussels-specific.

What do you need?

Money and dates

Flats that are free too late for your move-in are marked as a problem.

Where do you work?

Commute times are estimated to the EUROCONTROL site in Haren. For another workplace, the commute numbers will not be right yet.

Which areas?

Tick the areas to search. More areas means a longer search.

Almost done

When you press Finish, your answers are saved and the page is rebuilt.

Next you can start the first search (about 10 minutes the first time). Optional extras (Gemini, a shared copy for a friend, Gmail) are explained in the control panel.
