# The fun layer 🎭

Flat hunting is a long, boring, sometimes scary job, and the author did it with a friend in a hostel. The fun layer is there to keep two tired people going. It is **optional**, mostly silly and has some dark humor about being homeless. Press **🔊 Fun / 🔇 Quiet** at the top (or the `q` key) and all of it stops: sounds, voices, animations, pop-ups, visitors.

**What needs the shared copy:** the points, streaks, league, bingo, loot, cheers and the uploaded pictures are stored on the [shared copy](SHARED_COPY.md). Without it, those parts are paused and the Today tab says so. Sounds, voices, visitors, fake pop-ups and the other toys work without it.

All switches are in the **Fun settings** box at the bottom of the **🎉 Today** tab.

## The Today tab

- **Daily goal and streak:** a number of contacts per day (default 3) and a streak of days with at least one contact. Your face gets a hat as the streak grows: 🧢 1 day, 🦸 3, 👑 7, 🏆 14.
- **Team goal:** a bar toward a number of booked viewings (default 5).
- **10-minute timer:** "just try 10 minutes". Optional hold music. A small timer follows you on every page.
- **Weekly league** between the people sharing the search: 1 point per contact, 3 for a reply, 10 for a viewing. You decide the prize.
- **Rejection bingo** (every call result lights a square, three in a row wins), a **no counter** that celebrates rejections, and **loot**: a silly item for every logged contact (40 to find).
- **Cheer** and **Poke** buttons: send your partner confetti, or a dark nudge with a sad trombone.

## Sounds, voices and pictures

- Sounds are made in the browser (a cheer for a call, a sad trombone for a no, a fanfare for a viewing). Nothing is downloaded.
- The browser's own voice reads pep lines out loud, with a random funny pitch. Browsers often stay silent until you have clicked once.
- **Your own pictures:** upload your face and any memes you like in the Fun settings. "Yes" memes appear for replies and viewings, "no" memes for rejections and nags, "meh" ones for ordinary calls. The project ships **no** memes or photos; use pictures you have the right to use.
- **Dark humor:** a switch. Off gives gentle lines only.

## Random visitors (on every page)

Every few minutes (default 5, between 08:00 and midnight, never while you type) something happens: a face walks in and says a line, a face peeks from a corner, a landlord ghost drifts by with a NO sign, a fake breaking-news bar scrolls past, or a fake ad appears (tapping it tells your partner you are giving up). Also: a landlord in a dressing gown who says "sorry, already rented", fake chat messages from Mom or a pigeon, a "Landlord is typing…" bubble that never delivers, a cookie banner with two "Accept all" buttons, a delivery notice for your flat, fake reviews, a "give up?" dialog with only "No" and "Definitely no", and sound gags. There is a "summon now" list in the settings so you can try each one.

## Nagging and nudges

- **Idle nagging:** if you log nothing for a while with the page open (default 20 minutes), it nags you, a bit harsher each time. Daytime only, never during the timer.
- **Hourly reminders** from 09:00 to 18:00 as browser notifications (and on your phone through the shared copy). They stay quiet once you have done the day's contacts.
- **Sleep:** after 10 quiet minutes the page dims and your face falls asleep. Any movement wakes it.
- **Price jokes:** hover a price and, now and then, it says something rude about it.
- **Leave blocker:** when your mouse heads for the browser's top bar, an overlay tries to talk you out of it. No website can really lock you in; this is a persuasion attempt, and you can switch it off.
- **Countdown:** a box counting the days to a deadline you set (leave it empty to hide it). It gets more dramatic as the date nears.
- **Mood radio:** three no's in a row get a sad trumpet; a booked viewing gets a rave sting.

## Please be gentle with yourself

The jokes are about the situation, not about you. If the nagging stops being funny, turn it off: Quiet mode, or just the nagging switch. Finding a flat is stressful enough.
